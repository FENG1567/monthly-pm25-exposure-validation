# Figure captions (English)

Figure 1 | Historical validation across countries and tasks. Full histogram-based gradient boosting (HGB) models use strict aerosol optical depth (AOD) quality screening, log targets and station-cross-fitted smearing. Panels (A,B) show R² with and without meteorology; (C,D) show corresponding root mean square error (RMSE) in µg m⁻³. T1 denotes station grouping, T2 spatial blocking, T2 buffer the 50-km buffer, and T3 2022 forward-year validation. R² colors use a signed logarithmic scale; labels show untransformed scores. RMSE uses a separate linear scale. NA denotes an unavailable metric; hatched I denotes an ineligible estimate. A dagger marks estimates based on fewer eligible folds. MET denotes meteorological predictors.

Figure 2 | Future-year performance by meteorological profile and update policy. Panels (A,B) show United States R² and RMSE; (C,D) show Canadian results under strict-QA HGB full models. Solid lines/filled markers denote fixed-window models, and dashed lines/open markers denote rolling updates. Color and shape distinguish meteorological profiles. Lines connect observed evaluation years. The reference line marks 2023. The inset lists the four Canadian 2024 RMSE values that overlap at the main panel scale. RMSE is in µg m⁻³.

Figure 3 | RMSE increments from aerosol optical depth (AOD) and fire predictors. Panels compare (A) B+AOD minus B, (B) full minus (B+AOD), and (C) full minus B. Points and bars show estimates and simultaneous 95% max-|t| intervals across all 78 contrasts, using 10,000 Exp(1) station-multiplier draws. The family contains 26 United States/Canada task–meteorology units. Intervals exclude zero in favor of the added predictors in 24/26, 12/26 and 26/26 comparisons, respectively. Rows group 13 country–task combinations with meteorological profiles offset. Negative ΔRMSE favors the added predictors; units are µg m⁻³. B denotes the base model with location, population and seasonal predictors. MET denotes meteorological predictors. T1 denotes station grouping, T2 spatial blocking, T2 buffer the 50-km buffer, T3 2022 forward-year validation, and T4 fixed-window future-year evaluation.

Figure 4 | Error and observation retention in high-fire-proxy months. (A) Full-model RMSE for P90 fire-proxy and other months across fixed-window country-years. (B) Retention of 2023 high-fire station-months under distance <50 km combined with non-geographic training min–max or P01–P99 bounds. The unrestricted sample is a reference. Canadian models with meteorology retain 466/725 observations (64.3%) under min–max and 302/725 (41.7%) under P01–P99 bounds. Rows group country/year in (A) and country in (B); color and offsets distinguish meteorological profiles. RMSE is in µg m⁻³; retention uses all eligible high-fire station-months as the denominator. P90 marks months above the regional fire-feature 90th percentile in the outer training set; P01–P99 denotes the training first-to-ninety-ninth-percentile range.

Figure 5 | Prediction-interval coverage and width in the primary United States/Canada evaluation. (A) Station-month coverage pools covered/tested station-months. (B) Whole-station coverage pools test stations whose entire observed sequence is covered, divided by tested stations. (C) Mean width is weighted by test station-month counts, in µg m⁻³. All 64 primary folds have finite calibration thresholds. The 16 country–task–profile groups contain five folds for T1/T2 tasks and one for T3 2022 forward-year validation. Meteorological profiles are offset within country/task rows; vertical reference lines mark nominal 90% coverage. Fold detail and the separate feasibility construction are in Supplementary Figure S2.

Figure 6 | Full-model and native monthly SatPM V6.GL.03 performance on matched station-months. (A) RMSE for the two estimates across 12 country–year–profile combinations: fixed 2023, fixed 2024 and rolling 2024. Filled circles denote the full model and open squares SatPM. (B) Paired ΔRMSE (full minus SatPM) with station-cluster bootstrap 95% intervals (500 resamples; seed 20261116). Negative differences favor the full model. The 2023 difference favors SatPM in the United States and the full model in Canada; all 2024 RMSE intervals include zero. Rows group country, year and update policy, with meteorological profiles offset. Units are µg m⁻³.
